Curious about

I’m Sangi Morgan, a Brooklyn-based UI/UX designer and recent design student. I care about the small details that make a digital experience feel considered, useful, and kind.

I came to design through a fascination with everyday rituals: the apps we open without thinking, the moments we need a little guidance, and the thoughtful choices that help technology fit into real life.

A SMALL STUDY IN BEING HERE

A way of working

Thoughtful from
the first question.

01

Listen closely

Understand the context, notice what people actually do, and ask better questions before jumping to solutions.

02

Find the thread

Make sense of the messy middle. Bring research, constraints, and possibilities together around the real need.

03

Make, learn, repeat

Prototype early, invite honest feedback, and let each iteration make the experience a little clearer.

04

Care about the details

Polish the language, states, and accessibility details that make a useful product feel complete.
